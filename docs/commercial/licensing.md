
Enterprise licensing, Competing Use grants, SLA support, and regulatory deployment assistance for AIDflow.

AIDflow is available under the **Functional Source License (FSL 1.1)** for internal production use, academic research, education, and bespoke consulting delivery.

Organizations building a **competing commercial product** on top of AIDflow or regulated institutions requiring **guaranteed SLAs, IP indemnification, and regulatory validation support** require a commercial license.

## What the commercial license covers

- **Competing Use Grant**  Overrides the FSL non-compete restriction, permitting you to embed AIDflow into a commercial SaaS product, scoring API, or multi-tenant bureau offering.
- **Enterprise Governance & Indemnification**  Commercial warranty, intellectual property indemnification, and formal vendor compliance documentation required by bank and insurer procurement teams.
- **Production Support & SLAs**  Guaranteed response times, priority bug fixes, security patches, and structured version upgrade assistance.
- **Regulatory & Model Validation Support**  Assistance with model risk management (MRM) packaging, statistical audit trails, and compliance documentation for regulated environments.
- **Custom Extensions & Integration**  Prioritized roadmap input or negotiated engineering for custom workers, encoders, and orchestration integrations (Ray, Airflow, Kestra).

## Who it's for

- **Credit Bureaus & Data Providers** launching commercial scoring or risk-API products

- **Regulated Banks & Insurers** requiring vendor SLAs, indemnification, and MRM audit support for internal deployment
