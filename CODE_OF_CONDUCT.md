# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Protecting personal, confidential, and security-sensitive information.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, intimidation, personal
attacks, discriminatory language, publishing another person's private
information, and intentionally disrupting discussion.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may
also temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they
consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact: **conduct@aigensys.com** (or open a private
issue via the repository's "Report" button). Do not use a public issue for
sensitive reports. Reports are reviewed within 48 hours and handled with
confidentiality to the extent reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is
representing the project in public spaces.
